# **🧭 What *Parens Patriae* Means**

Parens patriae \= “parent of the nation.”

It allows a government to sue or intervene **on behalf of the public** when:

* there is a **quasi‑sovereign interest**,  
* the harm affects a **broad class of citizens**,  
* the injury is **not merely private**,  
* and the government is the **proper party** to vindicate that interest.

This doctrine is **not** about protecting a single individual’s private rights unless those rights implicate a broader public interest.
